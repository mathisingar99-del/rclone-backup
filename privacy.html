Privacy policy

Personal rclone Backup is a personal setup operated by the Google account owner on their own computer. This policy describes that setup.

Google data access and use

rclone uses the owner's Google authorization to access file and folder metadata, upload selected local files to the owner's Google Drive, and compare uploaded files with local originals. This access is used only to perform the owner's file transfers and verification.

Storage

Uploaded files and their metadata are stored in the owner's Google Drive under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy. OAuth tokens and client configuration are stored in the local rclone configuration. Local transfer logs may contain file names, paths, and transfer details.

Sharing

This setup does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or AI model training, or send it to a separate application server. Data is exchanged with Google as necessary for the requested Drive operations.

Owner control

The owner can stop rclone, remove local configuration and logs, delete uploaded files from Google Drive, and revoke the app's Google account access. Revoking access does not delete files already uploaded to Google Drive.

This website

These static information pages do not request Google credentials or OAuth tokens and include no analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary website request data under its own policies.
